What we look for

US-market packs, identified by UPC

We buy the version of a product made for American shelves: US labelling and a US barcode. Where an item was made matters to us too, so tell us the country of origin printed on the pack.

Coffee and food Supplements and sports nutrition Pet food and pet care Household cleaning Personal care Confectionery
  • BarcodeThe UPC on the pack matches the US-market product.
  • LabelsOriginal US labels in English, sealed and undamaged.
  • DatesA clear best-before or expiry date. Short-dated stock is welcome if the date is stated.
  • QuantityFrom a few cases to full pallets.
  • LocationAnywhere in the world, including the US. We agree collection or shipping for each order.

How it works

From your list to a written offer

  1. Stop 1

    You send a list

    Your catalog, a stock sheet, or a reply to our wanted list.

  2. Stop 2

    We make an offer

    Within two working days: the items we want, quantities and price.

  3. Stop 3

    We agree terms

    Price, payment and shipping are confirmed in writing before anything moves.

  4. Stop 4

    Goods move

    Stock is collected or shipped and paid for as agreed.
