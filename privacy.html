How we use contact details

Shelfstone contacts businesses about buying their stock. This page explains which details we hold, why, and how to stop hearing from us.

What we hold

  • Business contact details that companies publish themselves, for example on their own website or a brand's list of distributors: company name, country, website, business email and phone.
  • What you send us: stock lists, prices, photos and our email conversations.

Why we hold it

To contact businesses that may hold US-market products, and to make and manage offers. This is our legitimate business interest. We do not sell or share these details, and we do not use them for anything else.

How to stop hearing from us

Reply "no thanks" to any email from us, or write to george@shelfstone.com. We will stop contacting you and remove your details, keeping only a note not to contact you again.

How long we keep it

Contact details for as long as we may do business together, and records of completed orders for as long as tax law requires.
