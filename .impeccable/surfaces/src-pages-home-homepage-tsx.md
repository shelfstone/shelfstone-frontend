# Home page

Scope: the one-page home (hero plus sections 01–05), header and footer. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: export and sales staff at distributors, wholesalers and importers worldwide, arriving from George's email or on their own. Job: decide whether Shelfstone is a real buyer, then email an offer or a full stock list. Proof on hand: the wanted list by UPC, the field list, the process, one named contact. No photos, company details, clients or testimonials exist; placeholders stay visible.

Constraints: keep all current copy word for word, the Shelfstone name and bar logo, the one-page section order. Nothing flashy or playful.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Shelfstone's printed buying catalog, an edition-dated list of exactly what we buy, by code. It refuses the SaaS hero-plus-feature-cards page and the photo-led sourcing-agent template.

OWN-WORLD: White coated stock, near-black ink, one spot blue (#005eb8 family) used only for actionable things and wanted codes. Condensed grotesk display set tight, workhorse grotesk text, tabular figures. Running header with edition stamp and page number, ruled item tables with a 4px rule over a hairline, solid spot-blue order slip, thumb-index tabs down the right edge. One off-centre code rail every column registers to; one row unit for heights and gaps.

STORY: In one screen the visitor sees who we are, the exact UPCs we want, and the email. Scrolling reads like turning catalog pages: how it works, what we look for, the full wanted list, what to put in your list, who we are.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Running header (logo, "Buying list · Edition <date>", page number) over a 2px rule. Left rail (~200px) with kickers and a large blue "UPC". Right: the three-line headline at near full width, lede, then a "Now buying" table of the first three UPCs beside the blue order slip carrying george@shelfstone.com, which is the primary action. Thumb-index tabs fixed down the right edge, "Cover" active.

FORM: Wholesale trade catalog, position 3 of 7 on the grounded list (assigned by the roll). Signature interaction: the thumb-index tracks scroll, the current section's tab fills spot blue, and clicking a tab turns to that page. seed:cc77e19431d0f606

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
